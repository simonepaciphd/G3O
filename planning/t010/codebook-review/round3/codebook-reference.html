What the page says

page verdict page_verdict

Question: Code this PAGE for the named institution only, using only what the page says. Does it document a generative-AI activity at this institution? If several values fit, take the first in this order.

confirms_activityThe page documents a specific generative-AI activity AT THIS institution: its own GenAI tool, pilot, policy or guidance on GenAI, procurement, deployment, or institution-directed staff GenAI training. The text must be explicitly generative AI (LLMs, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, AI chatbots, AI-generated content or equivalents) and tied to this institution, not only to a national programme or another body. The activity must be carried out or adopted by the named institution itself. Exclude activity by a parent, higher-level or neighbouring body (e.g. a district, county, regency, region, consortium or city department when the named institution is a village, ward or sub-unit), by a similarly named organisation or company, and pages by vendors that only use the place name. Generic 'AI', 'artificial intelligence', 'smart' systems, automation, analytics, prediction, transcription, translation, image or video recognition, or traditional machine-learning tools do not count unless the page says the tool generates text, images, audio, code or other content, or names a generative product or model (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, an LLM, an AI chatbot or AI assistant).
ambiguous_genaiThe page mentions AI at this institution, but it cannot be settled whether it is generative AI (e.g. a generic 'AI strategy', 'AI tools', analytics, traditional machine learning).
ambiguous_attributionThe page mentions generative AI, but it cannot be settled whether THIS institution is involved (e.g. a country-wide or regional GenAI initiative without confirmation that this institution takes part, or a page about a similarly named body).
other_bodyThe page documents a generative-AI activity, but it clearly belongs to a different body, not the named institution: a parent, higher-level or neighbouring body (e.g. a district, county, regency, region, consortium or city department when the named institution is a village, ward or sub-unit), a similarly named organisation or company, or a vendor or marketing page that only uses the place name.
event_onlyThe only GenAI content is a public-facing event, workshop, seminar, showcase or educational programme, with no documented adoption, procurement, deployment or governance by the institution itself (WS10 D1: not a qualifying activity).
background_onlyThe page gives only general context (national AI strategy, vendor overview, news about GenAI in general) and documents no activity at this institution.
confirms_absencev2.5 `confirms_absence` (page states absence; distinct from `no_genai`, no relevant content)
no_genaiThe page contains no generative-AI content relevant to this institution.
not_assessable`not_assessable`, set mechanically when a page was not reviewed, never a classifier answer, not blocking institution `no` (C1 a), and left out of the base for institution-level rates

disclosure form disclosure_form

Question: What kind of document is this page?

register_cross_institutionThe page is, or is one entry in, a structured list of AI or algorithmic systems kept by a government body, with several entries in a common format (inventory, register, catalogue, algorithmic transparency record in a standard template), **and the list covers more than one institution** (a national or sectoral register, a standard-template record series across agencies).
register_single_institutionAs CX-10, but the list covers **one institution only** (e.g. a city's own table of its AI tools).
mandated_reportThe page is a document produced to meet a reporting obligation, such as a statutory annual-report section or a compliance or transparency report under a named rule.
formal_recordAn official one-off document of record from a government body: adopted policy or guidance, legislation, council or board minutes or resolutions, budget documents, procurement notice or award, audit or inspection report, **response to a freedom-of-information request** (CX-01.1, W5 = b).
informal_officialA government body's own communication with no reporting obligation and no standard format: press release, news item, blog, social post, speech, event page, service or FAQ page.
third_partyNot published by a government body: news, vendor, academic, NGO or policy organization. A register entry quoted in a news article is `third_party` (code the page, not what it quotes).
unknownThe page's kind cannot be determined from its text (e.g. an archived fragment with no header).

disclosure scope disclosure_scope

whole_of_governmentThe listing or document the page belongs to covers institutions across the government (all central agencies, or all units of a level).
multi_institutionIt covers several institutions, but not government-wide (a sector, a region, a group of agencies).
single_institutionIt covers one institution only.
unknownCoverage cannot be determined from the page.
