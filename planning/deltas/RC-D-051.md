> Should the URL '{url}' be kept for further analysis? ## Cost (estimates; HANDBACK, "Cost implications")

- **Identity fields:** a few to about 20 tokens per page-classifier request at $0.042 per million input tokens: well under $0.01 per 704-unit recording. They may move verdicts and so change how many pages go on to extraction and X35 (the main cost); direction unknown.
