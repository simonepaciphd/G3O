## Approved replacement

Lowercase the entire extracted email address (local part and domain) before deduplication; count case-only variants once per actual page. Keep full normalized addresses internal and publish only domain/count pairs. Do not collapse aliases or plus-addresses; supporting quotes remain verbatim.

Example: Alice@EXAMPLE.org, alice@example.org and Alice@example.org become one internal address alice@example.org and public [{"domain":"example.org","count":1}]. Distinct bob@example.org adds a second address. alice+news@example.org remains distinct from alice@example.org. Repeat appearances and reuse of one actual page across activities do not multiply the page count.

This replaces RC-D-009 S2a's domain-only normalization/local-part case preservation, effective now. It does not retroactively make the earlier implementation compliant with that earlier contract. INT-001's former T-014 case-preservation fix is SUPERSEDED by this approved decision, not reported as an implemented repair.
