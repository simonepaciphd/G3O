## Definitions recorded

**Quote match rule (S1).** A supporting quote matches its source when at least 80% of the quote's words, in the original language of the page, are present in the source text (main or chrome text, R-042 r2#48). Text may only be removed for conciseness or reformulated for clarity. The rule tests presence, not entailment (R-042 unchanged on that point).

**Calibration label (P1 follow-up).** For T-003, a candidate pair is labelled a true match if jev scores it 4 or 5 on a 1-5 scale ("how accurately does the quote represent the source"), or if the 80% rule holds. "and/or" is read as inclusive or. If the 80% rule's scores do not correlate strongly with the jev scores, the rule is revisited by the PI. No numeric bar for "strongly" was set. T-003 reports the agreement table and a rank correlation for the PI to judge.

**Fixture labels (P4 follow-up).** T-040 real-page fixtures get their expected values from an agent labeller, scored by jev on the same scale. They are no longer PI-labelled. In T-041 the PI still rules on fixture failures, and the paid-run gates are unchanged.
