## What was wrong

Found by the exploratory analysis of the remaining misses after the C2 readout (`miss-analysis/C-after-classifier/REPORT.md`; PREREG A38 (7): exploratory, not evidence). In all five recorded settings of C2 the same five units lost an activity to `activity_span_unresolved`.

1. **Unicode form.** The matcher compared characters as stored. A page whose text is in decomposed form (a letter followed by separate accent marks) did not match an anchor the extractor returned in composed form, though both read the same. Unit: c2-0359 (Viet Nam; page text not in NFC form, 256 combining marks). The quote check did not have this defect: it normalises both sides first.
2. **Stricter than the quote check.** The quote check accepts a quote when at least 80% of its words are on the page (R-042, SPEC:114) and ignores case and punctuation. The matcher demanded the anchor character for character (up to whitespace). So one miscopied word, a changed capital or a dropped bracket lost the whole activity, although the same words passed as a quote. Units: c2-0593 (Estonia; 8 of 9 anchor words equal, the last word miscopied), c2-0357 (Germany; 7 of 8, "lernte" for "lernten"), c2-0292 (Indonesia; the page has "SPPG)", the anchor "SPPG"), c2-0633 (Viet Nam; page "Tổ chức", anchor "tổ chức").

## The rule as built (scope ruled "a", section 17; as on `2b5db6f`)

The matcher tries four steps in order and stops at the first that finds the anchor:

1. exact match (unchanged);
2. match up to whitespace (unchanged);
3. new: match after Unicode normalisation (NFKC) and whitespace; the match must start and end at whole characters of the stored text; offsets are in the stored text;
4. new: the quote check's word rule made positional. Anchor and page are split into the quote check's own word tokens (NFKC, case folded, punctuation dropped). A place is a run of as many page words as the anchor has. It is accepted only when at least 80% of the words are equal position by position, **and** exactly one such place starts in the search range, **and** the word whose offset is used is itself equal (the first word of a start anchor, the last word of an end anchor), **and** the anchor does not stand elsewhere on the page exactly, up to whitespace or up to Unicode form. Otherwise the passage stays unresolved, as before.

Changes made after the independent code review (commit `6c22c27`, "the word rule yields to an anchor that stands elsewhere; kana and jamo pages keep the new steps"; tests in `6bf5ea0`):

- **The word step yields to an exact anchor elsewhere** (review F1). Before, an anchor that stood exactly outside the search range (for example a templated sentence naming another ministry) did not stop step 4 from taking a near copy inside the range, so the passage could mark another body's sentence. Now step 4 returns nothing when the anchor stands anywhere on the page exactly, up to whitespace or after NFKC (`_stands_on_page`, `parser_v27.py:288-293`, called at `:314`).
- **Half-width kana voiced marks and compatibility jamo** (review F2). One such character on a page switched steps 3 and 4 off for the whole page (it failed closed: no wrong offset). Now a character whose own NFKC form begins with a combining mark or a conjoining jamo is grouped with the one before it (`_joins_previous`, `:207-211`), so those pages keep both steps.
- **The page index is built once per page** (review F3, cost only): `_page_index` and the whitespace index are cached for the last two page texts (`:252-265`). No result changes.

What follows from step 4, to know before approving:

- It is never wider than the quote check: anything it accepts has at least 80% of its words on the page.
- **It also resolves anchors that differ only in case or only in punctuation**, because those are inside the quote check's normalisation. This is wider than "one miscopied word" as section 15 worded it; the PI ruled it in (section 17, "a"). On C2 the word step resolves four units (c2-0593, c2-0357, c2-0292, c2-0633); two of them (c2-0292, c2-0633) differ in punctuation or case only.
- An anchor under five words must have every word equal.
- Refused by design: two qualifying places; a slip in the first word of a start anchor or the last word of an end anchor; a missing or added word; a word split by a hidden character; an anchor that starts inside a word run in a script written without spaces; an anchor that stands elsewhere on the page as written.
- A passage found by step 4 runs from the first word to the last word, so punctuation just outside is not in it (one C2 unit, c2-0357, ends before the full stop).
- Which step found a passage is not stored (backlog B-19).
- Zero-width characters and soft hyphens are not ignored, by the matcher or by the quote check. No C2 anchor is lost to them (backlog B-15).

An anchor the old matcher found keeps its offsets: steps 1 and 2 are the old code and run first.
